Privacy Policy

Version 2026-09 · Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information KASH collects, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. KASH is operated by KASH General Suppliers ("we", "us"), Nairobi, Kenya. We are the data controller for the information described here.

1. Who this applies to

KASH is a business tool for staff and managers. You must be 18 or older to hold an account. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18; if you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

2. What we collect

InformationWhy
Your name, email, phone number, role and divisionTo create your account and control what you can see and do.
Your passwordNever stored as text. We keep only a salted, one-way hash.
Records you or your colleagues enter: trips, orders, bookings, payments, expenses, messages, remindersThis is the business data KASH exists to hold. It can include customer and guest names, phone numbers and payment references.
Driver details entered by an administrator (ID number, licence, next of kin)Fleet and safety management.
When you accepted these terms, and which versionTo keep a record of your consent.
Security logs: sign-in successes and failures, role changes, IP addressTo detect and block misuse and to investigate incidents.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Who can see it

Inside your business, what each person sees depends on their role (for example, a Transport Manager sees only Transport). Messages are visible only to the two people in the conversation. Your administrators can see and manage the accounts of their team.

4. Service providers we rely on

  • Cloudflare hosts the app and stores the data. It may be stored and processed on servers in several countries; Cloudflare applies its own security and data-protection commitments.
  • Google Fonts serves the typeface. Your browser contacts Google, which can see your IP address.
  • OpenStreetMap supplies map tiles on the Transport map, so your browser contacts their servers when a map is shown.

Information may therefore be processed outside Kenya. We transfer data outside Kenya only to the providers above, to run the service, and with safeguards consistent with the Data Protection Act, 2019.

5. Cookies and browser storage

KASH does not set cookies and does not use advertising or analytics trackers. To work, it keeps three small items in your browser's local storage:

ItemPurpose
kash.session.v1Keeps you signed in (your session token and basic profile). Cleared when you sign out.
kash.prefs.v1Your display choices, such as the date range and density.
kash.themeLight or dark mode.

These are strictly necessary for the app to function, so no consent banner is needed. You can clear them at any time in your browser settings, which will sign you out.

6. How long we keep it

Business records are kept while the business's account is active. After an account is closed we delete or anonymise personal information within 90 days, unless the law requires us to keep it longer (for example, financial records for tax purposes: the period set by Kenyan tax law, currently five years).

7. How we protect it

Passwords are hashed; connections are encrypted; access is limited by role and enforced on the server; repeated failed sign-ins are blocked; and security events are logged. No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify affected people and the regulator of a serious breach as the law requires.

8. Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 you may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to certain uses, and receive a copy. Contact us using the details below. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (odpc.go.ke).

9. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the version above and ask you to accept it again.
