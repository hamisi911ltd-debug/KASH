Welcome to KASH

One trusted name for everything your business and home need

KASH General Suppliers is a Kenyan company that brings three kinds of service together under one roof. Instead of juggling a transporter, a food supplier and a place to stay, you deal with one team that knows your needs and answers the phone. Whether you run a restaurant, a shop, a farm, a school trip or a family visit to Nairobi or the coast, we have something ready for you.

Transport & logistics

Trucks for bulk cargo, saloon cars and Ubers for people and small loads, and tuktuks for quick local trips in Mombasa and beyond. Every vehicle is maintained, every driver is experienced, and every trip is tracked from pickup to drop-off.

Agro & food supply
