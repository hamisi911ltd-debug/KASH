Frequently asked questions

Is VAT included in the KASH rate card?

No. Rates are shown before 16% VAT, and VAT is added on the invoice.

Where does the trucking start?

From the Nairobi ICD, Syokimau CFS, Compact CFS, NICT CFS and Michellcotts CFS.
